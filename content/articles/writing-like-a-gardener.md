# Writing Like a Gardener

Not every piece needs to be a building. Some writing is gardening.

You plant a thought. You water it with reading. You prune the sentence that grew too wild.

## Practices

1. Keep a compost file for unfinished lines
2. Revisit notes after two weeks
3. Publish the 70% version

Short pieces are not lesser. They are seeds.

> A garden is never finished, only tended.
