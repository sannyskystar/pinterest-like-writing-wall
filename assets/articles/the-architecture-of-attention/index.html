The Architecture of Attention

Interfaces are not neutral. Every feed, every notification badge, every infinite scroll is a small piece of architecture telling your attention where to go.

The cost of convenience

When everything is one swipe away, depth becomes expensive. We optimized for speed of access and lost the ritual of choosing what to read.

Attention is not just a resource. It is the way we compose a life.
  • Design for pause, not just for flow
  • Make the reader choose
  • Leave white space for thought

The writing wall is an attempt to reclaim that choice: a slow, searchable collection you browse with intent, not reflex.
