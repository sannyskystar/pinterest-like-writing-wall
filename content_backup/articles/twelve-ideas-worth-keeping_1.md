# Twelve Ideas Worth Keeping

1. Work that compounds beats work that spikes.
2. Constraints are editing.
3. A good sentence is a good interface.
4. Publish to learn, not to prove.
5. Boring tools used well beat exciting tools used poorly.
6. Archive everything. You will forget.
7. Read with a pen.
8. The second draft is the real first draft.
9. No one needs another productivity system. They need fewer promises.
10. Beauty is a form of care.
11. Finish things, then make them better.
12. Keep the wall alive.

> Keep a wall, not a funnel.
